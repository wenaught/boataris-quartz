### Верования

Multiple Deities with leadership of the gods based on personal allegiances rather than Law.
There is no universal form of deity in the pantheon.
The native deities have two religious hierarchies, not based on alignment. This is an unusual structure, probably due to the partial assimilation of a foreign pantheon or based on two different types of gods. There might be a “Greater” pantheon and a “Lesser” pantheon, as one example.
